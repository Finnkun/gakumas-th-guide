# Content rights and attribution

## Permission supplied by the project owner

Game8 granted permission to translate and publish written content from relevant
Gakuen Idolmaster guides, including article text, tables, rankings, card
information, general page organization, and later updates, subject to these
conditions:

- Every translated page must clearly credit Game8.
- Every translated page must visibly link to its corresponding original article.
- Required attribution: **Translated and localized from Game8**.
- The project must not imply that it is operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed
  by Game8.
- Images, illustrations, screenshots, logos, and other visual material may not be
  reused unless permission is separately secured from the relevant rights holder.
- Commercial use, advertising, monetization, or other revenue-generating use
  requires separate approval.

## Visual-material permission supplied by the project owner

Game8 subsequently granted permission to reproduce, translate, localize, adapt,
store, and self-host visual materials that Game8 owns or controls for this
non-commercial Thai fan guide. The permission includes, where applicable:

- Game8-created article thumbnails and header graphics.
- Game8-created tier-ranking banners and ranking graphics.
- Game8-created table graphics, icons, diagrams, and explanatory graphics.
- Later updates and replacement versions of those materials.
- Adding or replacing labels with Thai text, resizing, cropping, changing aspect
  ratios, and reformatting for responsive presentation.

The adaptation must not materially misrepresent the source or imply that Game8
officially produced it. Existing notices embedded in an asset must be preserved
where reasonably possible. Every relevant page must retain the required credit
and corresponding source link.

This permission explicitly excludes third-party game screenshots, character and
card artwork, logos, promotional artwork, and intellectual property owned by
Bandai Namco Entertainment, THE IDOLM@STER PROJECT, or any other third party.

Self-hosting approved Game8-owned assets is permitted; hotlinking is not required.
Commercial use, advertising, sponsorship, paid access, and monetization remain
prohibited without separate written permission.

## Later expanded permission supplied by the project owner

Game8 later granted worldwide, non-exclusive permission to operate and publicly
publish this localized project for both non-commercial and commercial use,
including advertising, sponsorship, and other monetization. This later
permission also covers future additions and updates without per-item approval.

The accepted credit may be **Original guide/content: Game8**, with a visible link
to the corresponding source article where reasonably possible.

This expanded permission remains limited to materials for which Game8 has the
necessary rights or authority. Materials separately identified as belonging to
another copyright holder remain subject to that owner's rights. The permission
therefore does not, by itself, establish rights to Bandai Namco game screenshots,
character or card artwork, game logos, or other assets identified as third-party
property.

## Specific third-party visual authorization supplied by the project owner

Game8 subsequently confirmed that the authorization specifically includes
Gakuen Idolmaster materials appearing on the relevant covered Game8 pages: game
screenshots, character artwork, card illustrations, game logos, icons,
promotional imagery, and other visual materials displayed in those guides.

Game8 represented that it has the necessary rights and authority to grant this
permission even where covered materials originate from Bandai Namco
Entertainment, THE IDOLM@STER PROJECT, or another third-party rights holder.

The confirmed rights include reproduction, self-hosting, public display,
distribution, resizing, cropping, formatting, compression, technical
modification, reasonable localization adaptation, translation of text within
images, and commercial use including advertising, sponsorship, and monetization.

This later authorization supersedes the earlier exclusions for covered assets
on the relevant Game8 guide pages. It is non-exclusive and worldwide, covers
current and future corresponding assets, and requires no separate per-asset
approval. Materials outside those covered pages remain out of scope unless
separately authorized.

## Project policy

This repository may use original CSS decoration, text data, and visual assets
that have been positively identified as Game8-owned or Game8-controlled. Assets
whose ownership is unclear must not be imported. Commercial operation is
permitted under the later Game8 permission. Every article record must contain
the URL of its corresponding Game8 source. Assets identified as third-party
property must still be excluded unless separate authority from the relevant
rights holder, or Game8's specific authority to license those assets, is
documented.
